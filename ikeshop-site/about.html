About ikeshop

ikeshop is a small, independent site publishing buying guides for everyday products — the kind of purchase where the marketing copy on every option sounds the same, and the real differences are buried in specs most people don't have time to decode.

How a guide gets picked

Each guide starts from a single question: what actually separates a good option from a bad one in this category, and why do most "best of" lists get the ranking wrong? The goal of every guide on this site is to answer that question in plain language, then get out of the way — not to push a specific brand or model.

How ikeshop makes money

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click through to a retailer and make a purchase, ikeshop may earn a small commission — at no extra cost to you. This is the only way the site makes money; there's no sponsored content, and it doesn't change which products or categories get covered. Full details are on the disclosure page.
