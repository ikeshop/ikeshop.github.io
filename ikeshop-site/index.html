Why ikeshop exists

Most "top 10" product lists are written to rank, not to help — padded with categories nobody asked about and conclusions that don't follow from the specs above them. ikeshop picks one category at a time and explains the two or three things that genuinely separate a good option from a bad one in it, in plain language, before pointing you anywhere to buy.
