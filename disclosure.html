Affiliate Disclosure

ikeshop is a participant in affiliate advertising programs, which are designed to provide a means for sites to earn commissions by linking to retailers' products. This means that when you click certain links on this site and make a purchase, ikeshop may earn a commission on that sale — at no additional cost to you.

What this means in practice

  • Prices you pay are the same whether you use a link on this site or go directly to the retailer.
  • Affiliate relationships do not determine which products or categories are covered, and they do not change the buying criteria described in a guide.
  • Not every link on this site is an affiliate link — some are plain links to a retailer's page with no commission involved.

Why this page exists

This disclosure exists to comply with FTC guidelines on affiliate marketing (16 CFR Part 255) and the disclosure requirements of the affiliate programs ikeshop participates in. If you have questions about a specific link or recommendation, feel free to get in touch via the contact details on the About page.
